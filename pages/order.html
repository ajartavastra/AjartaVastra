Order Procedure

How to Place an Order

Placing an order with Ajarta Vastra is simple and personal. 1. Customers may connect with Ajarta Vastra via WhatsApp, phone call, or email using the contact details provided on the website. 2. Upon receiving your enquiry, we will confirm the availability of the selected product and share the payment details. 3. Once payment is received, the product will be dispatched through a suitable delivery service such as a reputed courier partner or India Post, as mutually agreed. Please note: Delivery charges, if applicable, shall be borne by the customer and will be communicated at the time of order confirmation.

Product Availability

All products displayed in our gallery are subject to availability. As many of our sarees are handwoven and limited in quantity, certain items may go out of stock without prior notice. We recommend confirming availability before making payment.

Mode of Delivery

Ajarta Vastra selects the most reliable and efficient delivery option available at the time of dispatch. The delivery partner may vary depending on location and service availability. If you have a specific delivery preference, please inform us at the time of placing the order so we can discuss suitable arrangements.

Incorrect or Damaged Product

We take utmost care in packaging and dispatching every product to ensure it reaches you in perfect condition. However, in rare instances, damage may occur during transit. If you receive a damaged or incorrect product: • Please inform us within 12 hours of receiving the product. • Kindly share clear photographs of the item and packaging for verification. After review, appropriate action — including replacement where feasible — will be taken on a case-by-case basis, subject to mutual understanding and return of the product in the exact condition in which it was delivered.

Returns Policy

We request customers not to return any product without prior communication and confirmation from Ajarta Vastra. Unauthorized returns will not be eligible for replacement or refund. As our products are carefully verified before dispatch, returns are generally not encouraged except in cases covered under the damaged/incorrect product policy above.

Pricing Information

We strive to ensure that all pricing information displayed is accurate and up to date. However, inadvertent errors or changes in pricing may occasionally occur. In such cases, Ajarta Vastra reserves the right to decline or cancel an order after informing the customer, should there be a discrepancy in pricing.
