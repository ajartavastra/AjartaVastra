Shipping, Returns & Refund Policy

Shipping Policy

Ajarta Vastra strives to ensure that your selected products are delivered safely and promptly. • Orders are dispatched after confirmation of payment. • Shipping is arranged through reliable courier partners or India Post, depending on location and service availability. • Delivery timelines may vary based on destination and courier services. • Shipping charges, if applicable, will be communicated at the time of order confirmation and shall be borne by the customer unless agreed otherwise. Once the product is dispatched, delivery is subject to the timelines and policies of the shipping provider.

Returns & Replacement Policy

We take utmost care to ensure that every product is thoroughly checked and safely packed before dispatch. Returns or replacements are considered only under the following circumstances: • The product received is damaged during transit, or • The product received is incorrect In such cases: • You must notify Ajarta Vastra within 12 hours of receiving the product • You must provide clear photographs of the product and packaging for verification • The product must be returned in the original condition in which it was received After verification, replacement will be provided subject to product availability and mutual agreement.

Non-Returnable Situations

Returns or refunds will not be accepted in the following cases: • Return initiated without prior approval from Ajarta Vastra • Product returned in used, altered, or damaged condition after delivery • Change of mind after purchase As most handloom sarees are unique and limited in availability, returns are generally discouraged unless due to damage or incorrect delivery.

Refund Policy

Refunds, where applicable, will be processed only after: • Receipt and inspection of the returned product • Approval of the return request Refund timelines may vary depending on the payment method used. Ajarta Vastra reserves the right to evaluate each case individually to ensure fairness to both customer and artisan.
